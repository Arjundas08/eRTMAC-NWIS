The Industrial Challenge

Drilling Decisions Depend on
Knowledge That's Scattered and Inaccessible

Historical drilling knowledge lives across thousands of well completion reports, daily drilling reports, PDF documents, and individual experience. Retrieval is time-consuming and dependent on institutional memory.

Scattered Reports

Daily drilling reports, well completion reports, and mud logs exist across disconnected file systems, paper archives, and individual memories.

Time-Critical Decisions

When encountering unexpected formations or drilling events, engineers have minutes — not hours — to decide on operational responses.

Geological Complexity

Subsurface geology varies across fault blocks and formations. A nearby well's experience at the same measured depth may be geologically irrelevant.

Institutional Memory Loss

Experienced petroleum engineers retire. Their knowledge of specific wellbore failures, successful mitigations, and geological nuances disappears permanently.

NWIS transforms this fragmented knowledge into structured, verified, actionable intelligence
