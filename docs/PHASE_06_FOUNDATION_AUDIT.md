## 4. Architectural Boundaries for Phase 06 (NWIS PULSE)

To satisfy industrial drilling integrity standards:
1. **Strict Read-Only Architecture:** NWIS PULSE connects to telemetry streams as an **ingestion, analytical, and advisory system only**. Under no circumstances does NWIS issue control commands to mud pumps, top drives, drawworks, or blowout preventers.
2. **Three Visible Source Modes:**
   - **Mode 1 (`AUTHORIZED_LIVE`):** External connection to authenticated WITSML/ETP or industrial OPC-UA servers (pending operator authorization).
   - **Mode 2 (`GENUINE_RECORDED_REPLAY`):** High-resolution playback of authentic historical drilling logs from the Volve dataset.
   - **Mode 3 (`SYNTHETIC_DEMO`):** Stress testing, sensor fault injection, packet corruption, clearly watermarked in yellow/amber in the UI.
3. **No Unvalidated Universal Thresholds:** Rig conditions vary by hole size, lithology, and drilling fluid. Anomaly thresholds are parameterized, versioned in `engineering_rule_versions`, and must be validated per hole section.
4. **Two-Layer Evidence Passport:** Advisories cannot be vague alerts. They must display:
   - **Layer A:** Live telemetry evidence (channels, values, units, timestamp, quality flag).
   - **Layer B:** Historical offset evidence (offset well, event narrative, exact report citation, formation depth relative alignment).
