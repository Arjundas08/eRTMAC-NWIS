## 3. GeoCore Stratigraphic Alignment

When a telemetry packet arrives with measured depth $\text{MD}$:
1. The **Trajectory Engine** interpolates inclination and azimuth using the definitive survey, computing True Vertical Depth ($\text{TVD}$) and Subsea Depth ($\text{TVDSS} = \text{TVD} - \text{KB}$).
2. GeoCore compares $\text{TVDSS}$ against validated `FormationInterpretation` boundaries to determine:
   - Active formation name.
   - Depth above/below formation top.
   - Formation penetration percentage.
3. Retrieves nearby historical offset incidents (within the same formation or $\pm 100\,\text{m}$ TVDSS window).

## 4. Operational Anomaly Rules

Engineering rules are versioned and approved by drilling superintendents:
1. **Kick / Gas Influx Rule (`v2.1`):**
   - Condition: $\text{Flow Out} > 112\%$ or sudden pit volume gain ($> 1.5\,\text{m}^3$).
   - Severity: `CRITICAL`.
2. **Lost Circulation Rule (`v2.0`):**
   - Condition: $\text{Flow Out} < 85\%$ or pit volume deficit ($> 2.0\,\text{m}^3$).
   - Severity: `WARNING`.
3. **Mechanical Packoff / Sticking Rule (`v1.8`):**
   - Condition: Torque surge ($> 32\,\text{kN}\cdot\text{m}$) accompanied by SPP increase ($> 15\%$) and ROP reduction.
   - Severity: `WARNING`.

## 5. Two-Layer Evidence Passport

Every advisory generated by NWIS PULSE is bound to two inspectable evidence layers:
- **Layer A (Current Telemetry Evidence):** Triggered channel, measured value, units, delta from baseline, standpipe pressure, pit volume, and data quality state.
- **Layer B (Verified Historical Evidence):** Offset well identity, historical incident type, report citation, exact report passage, and stratigraphic alignment.
